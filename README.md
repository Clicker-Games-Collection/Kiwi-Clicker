## How to Play

Your journey in **Kiwi Clicker** begins with a simple click on your kiwi bird to produce kiwis. Each click yields one kiwi, which you can deliver to the king for coins. However, each delivery faces a security check, adding an exciting layer of strategy. Speed up your delivery process to ensure the king gets his kiwis on time!

## Key Features

- **Simple Clicking Mechanics**: Start by clicking to produce kiwis and gradually increase your output as you earn coins.
- **Upgrades and Enhancements**: Invest your coins in upgrades that boost your clicking power and delivery speed. With smart investments, you can produce multiple kiwis per click, reaching hundreds or even thousands!
- **Unlock Unique Items**: Progress through the game to unlock exciting items like bag stuffers and bird power, further enhancing your kiwi production.
